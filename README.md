# 🧠 Chapter-Based Project Teaching Model

## Core Idea

Projects are **not for building features** — they are for **reinforcing understanding** in a controlled, structured way.

---

## 📚 Structure & Timing

- Switch to **chapter-based projects** instead of weekly ones
- Each project reflects **only what was taught in that chapter**
- Keep projects **short (max ~3 days)** to maintain focus and momentum
- **Reduce total number** of projects to avoid overload

---

## 🎯 Learning Philosophy

- **No new concepts** during projects (only reinforcement of learned material)
- Lessons should **naturally build toward** the project
- Instructor **explains logic and architecture before coding**
- **Focus on understanding**, not just completing tasks

---

## 👨‍🏫 Instruction Approach

- First projects are **instructor-led** to set standards and reduce confusion
- Use **checkpoints during the project** (not just final review)
- **Require planning before coding** (structure, state, data flow)

---

## 👤 Student Experience

- **Prioritize individual learning** over team logistics
- Projects should be **mostly solo**, especially early on
- **Ensure equal hands-on practice** for all students
- Avoid situations where advanced students dominate complex tasks

---

## 🤝 Teamwork (Balanced Approach)

- Introduce teamwork **gradually**, not immediately
- When used, **assign clear roles** and rotate responsibilities

🧩 Project Design

Break projects into clear phases (planning → building → refining → presenting)

Define “minimum requirements” vs “bonus features” clearly

Project instructions must be extremely clear and structured

Include very simple examples to demonstrate expectations

👉 Example:

Instead of saying: “Build a gallery”

Say:

“Display a list of 3 items with title and image”

“Add a button that saves one item to localStorage”

Show a basic sample UI or data structure

---

## 🧠 Understanding & Accountability

- Students must **explain their code** during presentations
- **If they can't explain it, it doesn't count as learned**
- ✅ AI usage is allowed, but **understanding is mandatory**

---

## 🔄 Reflection & Improvement

Include a **short reflection after each project**:

- What was difficult?
- What did you learn?
- What would you improve?
